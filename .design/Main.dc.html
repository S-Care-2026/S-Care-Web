How it works

The band talks over an MQTT broker, not a raw HTTP round-trip — lighter on the ESP32's radio and cheaper to run at scale.

Wearable band ESP32 reads MPU6050 & MAX30102, publishes vitals.
MQTT broker Publish/subscribe over one open connection — cheap on cellular data.
Backend & storage A subscriber writes readings to Postgres, InfluxDB and Redis.
Web dashboard Caregivers watch it live, the instant it lands.
