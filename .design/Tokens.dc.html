Vital Green — theme tokens

The same palette that ships in the mobile app's lib/core/theme/app_colors.dart, carried over unchanged so a caregiver reading a fall alert on their phone sees the exact same red on the web dashboard. Three rules: no alpha compositing (every tint is a solid hex), no gradients or shadows, and every foreground measured against the surface it sits on. Ratios below are WCAG 2.1 contrast against the dark card #111A14.

Geometry — what “flat” means besides color

Corner radius8px 4px on chips and badges. Same radius on mobile cards and web sidebar/nav items.
ElevationBorders only Surfaces separate by a 3:1 outline, not a tonal step. No shadows, no gradient fills.
Status chipsSolid fill Filled block plus near-black label — 5.9–11:1 — instead of a tint with a border.
